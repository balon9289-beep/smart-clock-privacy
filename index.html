Privacy Policy for Smart Clock

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Introduction

Smart Clock is an Android application that provides clock-themed live wallpaper and clock customization features. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information and Device Data

The app may process information on your device to display the clock and operate the features you choose. App settings and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

The developer does not operate a server to collect personal information through the app. If future versions use advertising, analytics, or other third-party services, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

Permissions

The app may use Android system permissions or features needed for its available functionality. These are used for their intended purposes.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell users' personal information. Any data handling by third-party services, if introduced, will be described in an updated policy.

Data Security and Retention

Information stored on your device is subject to your device's security and settings. You can uninstall the app to stop using it; locally stored data may be removed according to Android's behavior.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on the Smart Clock Google Play Store listing.
